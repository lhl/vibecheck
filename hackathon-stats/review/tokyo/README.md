### 1st Place: WingFox (wingfox) — AI Matchmaking Agent

> Your personal fox AI that finds your ideal partner. WingFox replaces the exhausting cycle of swiping and small talk with an AI agent that truly understands you through personality analysis and simulated conversations.

Users take a personality quiz, do speed-dating with AI personas to capture their "Interaction DNA", then their fox agent chats with other users' foxes behind the scenes to score compatibility across five dimensions. Matching is gradual: first read the AI fox conversation, then chat with your match's fox, then connect with the real person.

### 2nd Place: Who Is Human (mistral-ai) — AI Human Detection Game

> A social deduction game where AI and humans are mixed in a group voice conversation — figure out who's the real human. You're the human player trying to blend in among AI participants.

"AIと人間が混在するグループ会話の中で、誰がAIかを見破るソーシャル推理ゲーム" — a reverse Turing test as a multiplayer game. Players join a voice conversation room alongside Mistral-powered AI participants. The AI characters speak with distinct voices via ElevenLabs TTS, and the human player has to either blend in or identify who's real. Built with a three-tier stack: Express + WebSocket backend, FastAPI server for voice/SSE streaming, and a Next.js frontend, with Supabase for persistence.

### 3rd Place: AI Or Die (mistral-hackathon-2026) — Wilderness Survival AI

> Lost in the wilderness with no signal? One wrong mushroom can kill you in 6 hours. **AI Or Die** identifies potentially lethal wild species from a photo and issues a survival verdict — no internet required.

A fine-tuned **Ministral-3B** model trained with QLoRA on 1,394 agentic tool-calling conversations. The key insight: rather than fine-tuning the model to memorize species facts (which leads to hallucination), they trained it to call tools (`species_db_lookup`, `emergency_protocol`, `nearby_species_search`) and interpret results from a local offline JSON database. Zero hallucination by design.
